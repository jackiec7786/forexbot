## 3. The method as the running bot trades it (BUY; SELL is the mirror image)

1. **Trend:** on closed 5-minute bars, price above its level **30 min and 2 h** ago → BUY. Below both → SELL. Otherwise **WAIT**.
2. **Position 1: 0.04 lots**, with a **$10 stop against it** (max loss about $40).
3. Position 1 **+$3** → its stop goes to entry **+ $0.50** → **once MT4 accepts it**, open **Position 2: 0.08 lots** (latest version: with a $1.50 starting stop).
4. As soon as price is $0.50 past Position 2's entry → **both stops move there**.
5. Position 2 protected and price **+$3 more** → **Position 3: 0.10 lots**. Stops go to Position 3's entry + $0.50, then rise **$3 with every further $3**.
6. **Exits:** profit stops; the **trend-reversal exit** (same 30 min / 2 h reading turns the other way → close positions without a profit stop); the **leftover rule** (Position 1 gone and a later position unprotected → close it, checked every tick).
7. **Session (broker day):** +10% → no new Position 1. **+25%** → close everything for the day.
8. Gold only, spread limit $0.60, at most one entry per 5-minute bar, never adds to a losing position, never a fourth position.

## 9. How to analyse a new statement

The MT4 Detailed Statement (`.htm`) lists every closed trade with its S/L at close. Useful split:
- **S/L in profit** (above entry for a BUY) = closed by a profit stop → a win.
- **S/L 0.00** = closed with no stop (trend exit, leftover rule or by hand).
- **S/L on the loss side** = closed by the Position 1 or Position 2/3 starting stop, or by the trend exit while that stop was set.

Group the trades by settings period, by size (0.04 = Position 1, 0.08 = Position 2, 0.10 = Position 3) and by exit type, then compare the profit factor and average win against average loss.
